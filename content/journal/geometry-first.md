Before the first carbon layer, there were months of drawings. We started with the rider: where the hands sit after five hours, how the weight moves over the front wheel on a descent, how much the bike should forgive when the road turns rough.

Only then did we draw tubes around that position. Stack and reach first, then the angles that make a bike feel calm at speed and alert in a hairpin.
