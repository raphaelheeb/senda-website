Choose SENDA Road if most of your riding is on tarmac and you like long climbs and fast descents. Choose SENDA Gravel if you leave the tarmac often, ride in all seasons, or want one bike for commuting and weekends.

Still undecided? A fitting at a partner studio is the best place to settle it.
