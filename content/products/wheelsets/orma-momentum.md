A hooked, tubeless-compatible rim chosen for modern road riding rather than one narrowly defined condition. The 24 mm internal width is designed around the wider tubeless tyres found on current road bikes.

Twenty straight-pull spokes front and rear, 3.8 mm carbon spokes, sealed hubs with steel bearings and a matte, scratch-resistant finish. The priority is dependable use, not specification-sheet theatre.
