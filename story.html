Our story

The path here.
Three decades in the making.

Senda is Romansh - Switzerland's fourth language, spoken in the high valleys and it means "path." This is ours.

Late 1990s · The foothills

Two friends who never stopped riding.

We're two childhood friends who grew up in the Alpine foothills and now live in Zurich and London. We fell for road cycling in the late 1990s - back when it was widely considered a sport for old men. We didn't care. One of us had a cousin racing professionally at the time, and watching him suffer up mountains on the road and on television made the whole thing seem less like a hobby and more like a calling.

We started participating in local races and cycling events. We rode through the Alps, mostly, and wherever else work or curiosity took us.

The building years

Steel, then aluminum, then carbon.

Because we couldn't leave well enough alone, we built our own bikes. First steel, then aluminum, then carbon - always chasing lighter, stiffer, better. One of us went on to study materials science, the other architecture. In hindsight, we were splitting the bicycle between us: one obsessed with what it's made of, the other with how it looks and feels.

2024 · The turn

The manufacturers behind the bikes you already know.

The idea for SENDA took shape in 2024. We spent months building relationships with long-standing OEMs in the bicycle industry - the engineers and factories behind some of the most respected frames on the market. We visited their facilities, tested their products, assessed their processes, and selected the ones we'd trust with our own builds.

"If we're this enthusiastic about it, why keep it to ourselves?"
Now · What we believe

Pay for the bike. Not the noise.

A bike should perform and look exceptional - not one at the price of the other. Cutting-edge materials and honest engineering matter more than marketing budgets. And when you buy a SENDA, you should be paying for the bike - not for middlemen, sponsorship deals, or a flagship store on an expensive street.

We're now finalising our first models. Along the way, we'll ask for your opinion on designs and specs, ride with you, and help grow the cycling communities we're part of. Because a path is better when it's shared.
