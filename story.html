Our story

Every great journey
starts with a path.

SENDA means path in Romansh, Switzerland's fourth national language. To us, it represents far more than a trail or a road. Every ride is a journey. Every decision is another step forward. And every brand is shaped over time, not created overnight. SENDA is the result of a journey that began nearly thirty years ago.

Late 1990s · Where it all began

Two friends. One shared passion.

We are two lifelong friends from the Swiss Alps, now based in Zurich and London.

In the late 1990s we discovered road cycling, long before it became the sport it is today. Since then, cycling has remained a constant throughout our lives.

Local races, alpine passes, multi-day tours and countless hours in the saddle have only reinforced one belief: a truly great bicycle creates a feeling that is difficult to explain—you simply have to ride it.

Learning by building

Understanding instead of simply buying.

Over time, riding was no longer enough. We wanted to understand what makes one bicycle feel different from another.

We began building our own bikes, experimenting with steel, aluminium and eventually carbon, while diving deep into geometry, materials and engineering.

One of us studied Materials Science. The other Architecture.

Looking back, we naturally divided the bicycle between us: one focused on engineering and manufacturing, the other on proportion, design and the details that make a bicycle feel complete.

Those two perspectives continue to shape SENDA today.

Why we founded SENDA

Why do so many premium bicycles still require compromises?

Some brands excel in design. Others in engineering. Some offer outstanding value but compromise elsewhere. Finding the right balance is surprisingly rare.

Too often, a significant part of the purchase price pays for marketing, sponsorships and complex distribution networks rather than the bicycle itself.

We knew those compromises well—because we had made them ourselves.

So we decided to find out whether there was a better way.

2024 · Building our own brand

In 2024, we began creating SENDA.

Over many months we visited development centres and manufacturing facilities across Asia and Europe, meeting the engineers and production teams behind bicycles built for some of the world's most respected brands.

We evaluated materials, manufacturing processes and quality standards—not in search of the lowest cost, but of partners who share our commitment to craftsmanship, precision and long-term collaboration.

Because exceptional products are built by people who care about getting every detail right.

What we believe

We believe cyclists should not have to compromise.

By combining our complementary backgrounds with long-standing relationships across the global cycling industry, we believe we can offer exactly that balance.

That is also why we sell directly.

Not because direct-to-consumer is fashionable, but because keeping our organisation lean allows us to invest where it matters most: engineering, materials, manufacturing quality and carefully selected components.

When you choose a SENDA, you should be paying for the bicycle—not for expensive marketing campaigns, flagship stores or unnecessary layers of distribution.

Experience over marketing

We don't believe advertising makes a bicycle better.

That's why we develop our bikes together with experienced cyclists and a small group of carefully selected ambassadors who share our passion and provide honest feedback.

Over time, we hope to create places where SENDA can be experienced in person—clubhouses and test centres where people can meet, ride together and discover our bicycles.

Until then, our most important testing ground remains the road.

The journey continues

Our first models are currently in their final stage of development.

As we prepare for launch, we welcome feedback on design, geometry and specifications.

Because the best journeys are rarely travelled alone.
