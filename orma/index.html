A wheelset is judged the same way a frame is: on the details you don't see until they matter.

01 —— Rim Engineering

A precise line.

Non-wavy, hooked and tubeless-compatible at a 45mm allround depth – deep enough to matter aerodynamically, shaped for stability in crosswinds and comfort over long days.

An internal width of 24.8mm is tuned for the wider tubeless tires modern road and gravel setups actually run.

02 —— Straight-Pull Build

Direct power, true wheels.

20H front, 20H rear, laced with FARSPORTS T-shaped bladed carbon spokes at 3.8mm. Straight-pull spokes keep power transfer direct and the wheel true, ride after ride.

03 —— Hubs Built to Last

Reliability over marginal grams.

H-WORKS HW288A hubs run on steel bearings – chosen for durability, not just a lighter spec sheet. Coating-free, scratch-resistant finish.

Rated to 120kg rider weight, because a wheelset should perform for every rider who trusts it.
