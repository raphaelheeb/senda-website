Three principles
behind Momentum.

A wheelset is not defined by one headline number. It is defined by how its details work together, kilometre after kilometre.

01 — Rim profile

A useful 45 mm.

A non-wavy, hooked, tubeless-compatible rim profile selected for modern all-round riding rather than a single narrow use case.

Its 24.8 mm internal width is designed around the wider tubeless tyres used on current road and fast-gravel bikes.

02 — Wheel build

Simple, direct, considered.

Twenty straight-pull spokes front and rear, using 3.8 mm T-shaped carbon spokes. A deliberately focused build for development, testing and refinement.

03 — Everyday confidence

Built for more than a perfect day.

Sealed hubs with steel bearings and a coating-free, scratch-resistant finish reflect the priority: dependable use, not specification-sheet theatre.

The current wheelset is rated to a 120 kg rider weight limit.
